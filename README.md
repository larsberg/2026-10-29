The scene starts with three nearby copies of a sculpture built from seven randomly sized, positioned and rotated boxes combined using [Manifold](https://manifoldcad.org/docs/jsapi/documents/Using_Manifold.html) CSG. **Mesh → Box union → Box spread** scales the seven box centers within each sculpture from 0× to 4× while preserving seeded sizes and rotations; 1× is the original spacing and 2× is the saved doubled spacing. Changing spread rebuilds the CSG surface, operation layers, colliders, joints, and skin binding. `src/box-union.js` converts the centered union into shared polygon vertices and triangle faces for the existing mesh operations, surface material and wireframe. Interior overlapping faces are removed by the union.

`src/sculpture-group.js` places the three identical copies in a compact triangle with a small gap based on the finished mesh bounds. The CSG and operation layers are computed once and copied; each sculpture has its own seven-body physics rig, skin, polygon-normal texture, and collision groups, for 21 rigid bodies total. Copies can collide and be grabbed independently. Mesh edits, Undo, visibility, material settings, and palettes apply to all three through the existing controls. Camera follow uses their combined bounds, shadow fitting includes every surface, and the initial camera distance is widened to frame the group. Reset and regeneration restore the triangle.

**Mesh → New variation** changes the saved random seed and regenerates all seven boxes. Reload and Reset reproduce the saved seed and spread. The saved pipeline runs Triangulate → Dialate → Hexelate → Smooth. Smoothing uses four passes at 0.5 strength; each layer remains adjustable in the existing drawer, and manual operations retain Undo. The union is cached by seed and spread, so resizing or changing operation layers does not repeat the CSG calculation.

`src/box-rig.js` binds the processed surface as a Three.js `SkinnedMesh` to seven bones. Each dynamic Box3D body uses a convex hull fitted after all mesh operations. `src/collider-fit.js` clips the finished solid to each source box's region, builds its convex hull in the bone's local frame, and samples at most 13 vertices to reduce mobile collision work and stay within Box3D's 255-half-edge limit. This also fits buried boxes that have no exterior vertices assigned to them. Empty regions have no collision shape. Sampling retains the axis extremes and stays inside the full hull; it never restores the old box corners. Hulls remain a rigid approximation of the deforming skin and fill concave recesses.

The original bone frames and cone constraints are retained. Spherical joints form a tree within each overlapping source cluster, preferring the largest overlaps, with 30° cone/twist limits and damped 2 Hz rotation springs. Only actual source intersections in the generated layout create connections: separated clusters remain unconnected, and isolated boxes have no joints. Changing spread or seed recalculates these connections. Each connected cluster has its own collision group: its intentionally overlapping hulls ignore each other, while detached pieces, other clusters, and outside bodies can collide with it. Contact does not create a joint. Up to four normalized influences per vertex blend by distance to the oriented source boxes within its nearest cluster; detached skin has no influence from unrelated bones. The GPU skins the surface while matching CPU deformation updates the polygon wireframe, polygon-normal texture, camera target, and shadow bounds. Editing, Undo, Reset, and regeneration refit and rebind the rig without accumulating physics objects.

Drag a fitted collider with the primary pointer to grab it at the hit point, then release to let it go. A temporary Box3D motor joint pulls that point toward the pointer on a plane facing the camera, allowing the body to rotate and the connected cluster to follow. Picking uses Box3D's native hull raycasts at the current physics poses, so the removed box corners cannot be grabbed. Drag empty space to orbit. Camera follow freezes during a grab. Pointer release/cancel, lost capture, focus loss, Pause, Reset, mesh edits, and disposal release the grab and restore orbit controls. The pointer routing is reused from `../2026-10-16/src/collider-drag.js`.

**Mesh → Physics → Show colliders** displays green hull-edge outlines through the surface using the same fitted points as physics. It is enabled in the saved setup, works with surface/wireframe visibility off, and changes visibility without resetting the simulation. Spread and collider visibility both use the existing Save/Reload workflow.

The wall plane generator, its controls, and its saved settings are removed. Loading older saves drops the wall fields. The café model remains in `src/cafe-setting.js` for reuse. The custom softbody solver, pin-noise coupling, Cannon solver, ground mesh and collider, and floor camera restriction are removed.

`src/physics.js` initializes `box3d-wasm/standard` 0.2.0, uses fixed 1/120-second steps, and follows play/pause, reset and focus suspension. `window.__meshSketch.physics` exposes the world and managed `addBody({object, body, shapes})` bindings. Shapes accept `type: 'box' | 'sphere' | 'capsule' | 'hull'` plus Box3D options. `window.__meshSketch.mesh.views[i].rig` exposes each sculpture's bones, bodies, joints, weights, and deformed positions; `mesh.rig` routes pointer grabs to the nearest collider across all copies. Gravity is saved at zero for a floating group; the existing gravity and friction controls remain. No invisible floor is installed.

Strudel, the scheduler, instruments and audio recording hooks remain. No score or musical lighting/noise mapping is loaded; audio starts off and remains silent until a new pattern is supplied through `window.__meshSketch.audio.setPattern(pattern)`. The previous compositions and their render script are removed.
